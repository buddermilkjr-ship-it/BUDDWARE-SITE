UNSEEN MASSACURE

Ashwood is almost empty. Something is wrong—and not everyone sees the town the same way.

IN DEVELOPMENT

ABOUT THE GAME

Unseen Massacure is a retro horror game set in Ashwood, a town where people have been leaving and the truth is harder to understand than it first appears.

Follow different characters as their stories unfold. Explore, uncover secrets, and discover what Ashwood has become.

This game is a work in progress. Story details, visuals, and gameplay may change during development.

FEATURES

• Retro-inspired visuals

• Story-focused horror

• Explore the town of Ashwood

• Multiple character perspectives
